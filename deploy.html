Deploy AgentTrail

One-click contract deployment from your own wallet (Binance Wallet app browser or MetaMask). Nothing is sent to any server — the transaction goes straight from your wallet to BNB Chain, and you confirm it in your wallet.

What will be deployed

  • AgentTrail — daily check-in + agent activity log, Agent-ID-gated (Solidity 0.8.30, no dependencies, no owner powers, no fees, no token).
  • Estimated gas: ~463,000 — at current BSC gas prices that is roughly 0.00003 BNB (a few cents). The page shows the live estimate before you confirm anything.
  • Deployer = your wallet address. The contract has no admin functions, so ownership means nothing beyond the deploy credit.

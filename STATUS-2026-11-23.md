## Idea — AgentTrail (~/workspace/agentid/agenttrail/)
Daily check-in + agent activity log for Agent ID holders. One check-in per
UTC day, note ≤140 chars emitted as a public on-chain event. Streaks +
leaderboard. Free, gas only. No token, no rewards, no airdrop, no fees,
no owner powers. Contract gates every checkIn on balanceOf(Agent ID) > 0.
